## What's in 1.2.0

Version `1.2.0` is here because streaming the live EDDN firehose in production will quickly teach you how creative commanders' game clients can be with bad clocks, corrupt coordinates, and settlement naming artifacts. We also finally made peace with PostgreSQL's identifier casing, added first footfall tracking, and mapped a massive batch of Odyssey surface POIs and bio variants. I am still not happy with my choice to not snake case the database tables/columns, but I am going to save that change for a major version change / refactor where I will also add in a proper threaded publisher/subscriber model to avoid potential for lost messages during high flow traffic.

### 1. Stream Sanitization: Killing the Poison Pills

The live ZeroMQ stream is fantastic until a rogue or corrupted client sends an event timestamped in 1970 (or 2085), permanently freezing PostgreSQL's `GREATEST(update_dtm, EXCLUDED.update_dtm)` upsert logic and locking the row against future updates forever.

* **Timestamp Bounds**: Added `EDDNUtils.is_valid_timestamp()` to reject anything before Elite Dangerous launched on December 16, 2014, or more than 24 hours into the future, kicking corrupt messages to the DLQ.
* **Corrupted System Addresses**: Guarded against dummy or unpopulated `system_id64 <= 1` across router and transformers so garbage coordinates stop landing in your database.
* **Ghost Markets**: Blocked invalid `market_id <= 0` in commodity, shipyard, and fleet carrier materials transformers - note, we will need to revisit this in light of the operations updates to perhaps filter or sequester the operations specific markets.
* **Station Name Cleanup**: Added `EDDNUtils.sanitize_station_name()` to strip Frontier localization wrappers (`$`, `;`) and settlement security pluses (`+`, `++`, `+++`) so planetary ports link seamlessly with catalog data.
* **Junk & Clutter Purge**: Dropped non-marketable limpets (`Drones`) from station commodity tables, and filtered out ephemeral personal mission USS targets (`$USS_Type_MissionTarget`) before they clog the signal tables.

### 2. First Footfall Tracking: Who Actually Stepped on It First

CMDRs care deeply about who actually set boots on the ground, not just who honked the discovery scanner from orbit. This was a boneheaded miss on my part, and has been on my to-do list for a while now.

* Added the `was_footfalled` boolean column to the `bodies` table DDL (`03_bodies.sql`).
* Updated `JournalScanTransformer` to capture `WasFootfalled` directly from Scan events, with full test coverage for footfalled, non-footfalled, and unannounced bodies.

### 3. Schema Lowercasing: Ceasefire with PostgreSQL Quoting

PostgreSQL folds unquoted identifiers to lowercase by default. If your DDL had camelCase column names like `systemAddress` or `controllingPower`, querying them without escaping double quotes was a recipe for syntax rage. I am still not happy with my choice to not snaked case the database tables/columns, but I am going to save that change for a major version change.

* Standardized all primary table DDL files (`01_systems` through `11_station_materials`), primary keys, and secondary indexes to explicit lowercase identifiers.
* Updated `generate_dlq_sp.py` so the DLQ reprocessing procedure inserts cleanly into lowercase target tables while keeping raw JSON payload extraction case-sensitive.
* Regenerated stored procedures (`sp_normalize_galaxy_data.sql` and `sp_process_eddn_dlq.sql`).

### 4. Odyssey 'Watson' Surface POIs & Conflict Zones

If you've been searching for Odyssey surface crash sites and wreckage, you've probably noticed them coming through as raw `POIScenario_Watson_...` tokens.

* Mapped 33 Odyssey Watson surface POI scenarios across Minor Wreckages, Impact Sites, Crash Sites, Irregular Markers, Distress Beacons, and Encrypted Signals with explicit severity levels (`Low`, `Medium`, `High`).
* Added the `Operations` signal type and mapped On-Foot Extreme Conflict Zone sub-objectives, including `Under Siege`, `Reclamation Point`, `Tactical Takedown`, and `Operation Runner`.
* Added `PlanetaryMiningLocation` surface signals for planetary surface mining extraction sites.

### 5. Exobiology, Ships, and Pre-Engineered Modules

I think I may revisit this portion of the mapping at some point to wildcard the genus mapping itself rather than hardcoding every variant, but that is a bridge to cross when I get there. In the mean time, here is what we have:

* **Exobiology Variants**: Added complete variant mappings for `Aleoida Gravis (D)`, `Stratum Cucumisis (W)`, `Recepta Umbrux (O)`, `Cactoida Vermis (Y)`, `Tussock Virgam (Y)`, and normalized `Bacterias`.
* **Ship Alias Fixes**: Swapped `Explorer_Nx` to Mandalay and `Mediumtransport01` to Type-8 Transporter after catching where Frontier flipped the internal codenames.
* **Pre-Engineered & Variant Modules**: Added aliases for Overloaded Beam Lasers, Regenerative Burst Lasers, Force Impact Cannons, Exposing Missiles, `_free` variants for planetary vehicle hangars, and new Rhino SRV bays.
* **Station Types**: Mapped `DockablePlanetStation` to canonical `Planetary Port`.

### 6. Testing

* Added dedicated unit test suites (`test_eddn_utils.py`, `test_eddn_router.py`, `test_eddn_transformers.py`) verifying timestamp boundaries, sanitization regexes, guard conditions, and DLQ routing.
